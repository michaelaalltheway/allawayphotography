Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, we recommend booking 8-12 months in advance. For other sessions, 4-6 weeks notice is ideal, though we can often accommodate shorter timeframes.

What's included in a session?

Each package includes the photography session, professional editing, and an online gallery for viewing and downloading. Specific details vary by session type.

Do you travel for sessions?

Yes! We're happy to travel for your session. Travel fees may apply for locations outside our local area.

When will I receive my photos?

Turnaround time is typically 2-3 weeks for standard sessions and 4-6 weeks for weddings. Rush delivery options are available.

What is your cancellation policy?

We understand plans change. Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to reschedule. Specific policies are outlined in our contract.

Do you offer prints?

Yes! We offer high-quality prints, albums, and wall art. Digital files are included with all packages.
